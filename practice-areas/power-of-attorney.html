Power of Attorney

A power of attorney is one of the simplest legal documents you can sign and one of the most powerful. It authorizes someone you trust to act on your behalf, whether for a single transaction or for the management of your affairs if you become unable to handle them yourself.

We prepare durable powers of attorney for financial and business matters, healthcare powers of attorney and advance directives, and limited powers of attorney for specific transactions. For entertainment clients, limited powers of attorney play a special role: touring artists, performers working abroad, and busy executives often need a trusted representative empowered to sign contracts, manage banking, or close deals while they are unavailable. We draft these instruments precisely, so your agent has exactly the authority you intend and no more, no less.

The right time to establish a power of attorney is before you need it. We help you choose the appropriate type, select and instruct your agent, and build in the safeguards that protect against misuse, giving you confidence that your affairs are covered wherever your career takes you.
