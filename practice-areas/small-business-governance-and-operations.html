Small Business Governance & Operations

Forming your company is only the beginning. Running it well legally, cleanly, and with good records is what protects you when disputes, audits, or opportunities arrive. Our small business practice provides ongoing counsel to keep your company compliant, and your interests protected as you operate and grow.

Our services include annual filings and state compliance, corporate minutes and record-keeping, amendments to operating agreements and bylaws, membership and shareholder changes, equity issuances and buyouts, employment and contractor documentation, policy development, and guidance on owner disputes and deadlock resolution. We also serve as outside general counsel for small entertainment companies, including but not limited to, labels, production shops and management firms, that need experienced legal guidance without the cost of in-house staff.

Many business owners neglect governance until a problem makes it urgent: a partner departs, an investor demands records, or liability protection is challenged because formalities were ignored. We keep your house in order year-round, so your company is always ready for scrutiny, investment, or sale.
