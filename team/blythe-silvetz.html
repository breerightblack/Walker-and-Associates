Blythe Silvetz is a communications and social media intern at Walker & Associates, where she supports the firm’s digital presence, content strategy, website updates, and promotional messaging for Broadway and entertainment-related projects. With a strong interest in entertainment, film, theater, and media, Blythe brings a creative and audience-focused approach to the firm’s communications work.

Her background in film, theater, and leadership has shaped her understanding of storytelling, branding, and audience engagement. She is especially tapped into using digital media to connect people during live entertainment, strengthening a company’s public voice, and bringing creative projects to wider audiences.

Outside of her work with Walker & Associates, Blythe is involved in film and theater as a student leader, performer, and creative collaborator. She plans to continue building experience in communications, entertainment, and performance while pursuing her passion for storytelling across film, theater, and the performing arts.
