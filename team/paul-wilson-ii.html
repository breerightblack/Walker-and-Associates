Paul Wilson II is a highly versatile Maryland-licensed attorney based in the DMV (District of Columbia, Maryland, and Virginia) area. Known for his eclectic counsel, Paul maintains a diverse and robust legal practice that spans high-profile entertainment litigation and transactional work, complex corporate finance disputes, and intellectual property disputes.

He is widely recognized across the firm for his rigorous legal research, analytical depth, and meticulous execution of sophisticated transactional and litigation strategies.

Attorney Wilson holds a Bachelor of Science in Arts Management from Indiana University Bloomington, and a Juris Doctor from Howard University School of Law. In his free time, Paul enjoys making music, cooking, and training Muay Thai.
