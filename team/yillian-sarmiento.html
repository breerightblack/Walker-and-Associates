Yillian Sarmiento is a litigation paralegal professional with more than 11 years of experience supporting attorneys and clients in a wide range of legal matters, including personal injury, business litigation, and civil disputes.

As Litigation Paralegal at Walker & Associates, she manages case administration, discovery, court filings, client communications, and day-to-day firm operations, helping ensure that every matter is handled efficiently and with attention to detail. Fluent in both English and Spanish, Yillian is passionate about making the legal process more accessible and providing compassionate support to clients during what are often challenging times in their lives.

Outside of the office, she is a devoted mother of three who understands the importance of balancing professional excellence with family, bringing both empathy and dedication to her work and the clients she serves.
