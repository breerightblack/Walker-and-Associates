Taja Nave is an incoming associate attorney, pending bar admission, with a background in transactional law, intellectual property, entertainment law, employment mediation, and corporate legal matters. She earned her J.D. from Emory University School of Law, where she completed the Transactional Law Certificate program and served as a member of the Emory Transactional Negotiations Team and BLSA Moot Court.

Taja’s legal training reflects both advocacy and business-focused experience. During law school, she was recognized as one of the top scholars in Emory’s Kessler-Eidson Program for Trial Techniques for her trial advocacy performance. She also received CALI Awards for earning the highest grade in several courses, including Legal Writing, Civil Procedure, Real Property, and Evidence.

Before and during law school, Taja worked with media, music, and entertainment companies in Atlanta, developing practical insight into the legal and business needs of creatives, entrepreneurs, and emerging companies. Her background in entertainment and business operations informs her client-centered approach to legal problem-solving.

In addition to her legal work, Taja serves on the board of an Atlanta nonprofit organization, where she supports community initiatives and organizational growth.
