Who We Are

Built on advocacy. Expanded for the way business moves today.

Walker & Associates was founded on a clear belief: clients facing high-stakes legal and business decisions deserve sophisticated, strategic counsel grounded in both experience and real-world understanding. Over the last 30+ years, the firm has built a strong reputation in entertainment law while steadily expanding its work across business, litigation, real estate, and strategic advisory matters.

What began as a respected Sports and Entertainment practice has grown into a broader platform serving companies, entrepreneurs, talent, production stakeholders, and decision-makers who need counsel that can protect their interests and help them move forward with confidence. Today, the firm advises clients at the intersection of business, entertainment, media, culture, contracts, disputes, and reputation-sensitive matters.

As Atlanta continues to shape the future of entertainment, business, and production, Walker & Associates remains positioned to serve both the creative economy and the broader commercial landscape around it.

That means supporting not only artists and filmmakers, but also founders, executives, investors, property owners, and organizations navigating complex legal challenges.

Who We Help

Clients at the intersection of business, media, and culture

Walker & Associates serves clients who operate at the intersection of business, media, and culture — from founders and executives to creators, production stakeholders, and brands navigating high-stakes legal and strategic matters.

Founders & Growing Businesses

We advise entrepreneurs, startups, and established companies on entity formation, contracts, partnerships, financing, intellectual property, and day-to-day legal infrastructure. Whether you’re launching a new venture, scaling operations, or managing complex agreements, we help you build a strong legal foundation for growth.

Film, Television, and Media Professionals

Our Film & Television division represents producers, directors, writers, crew members, production companies, and content creators in deal negotiation, rights acquisition, financing structures, distribution agreements, and other critical matters across the production lifecycle.

Artists, Musicians, and Talent

We continue to serve the creative community that helped build our reputation — including recording artists, songwriters, producers, managers, and other talent — with counsel on recording contracts, publishing, licensing, trademarks, and brand partnerships.

Executives & Decision-Makers

We work with C-suite leaders, investors, and key stakeholders who need strategic guidance on corporate governance, transactions, employment matters, disputes, and reputation-sensitive issues where legal and business risks converge.

Production Companies & Studios

From independent producers to larger production entities, we provide comprehensive support for development, financing, production services, talent agreements, post-production, and distribution across film, television, and digital content.

Agencies, Brands, and Cultural Organizations

We represent agencies, brands, and organizations active in entertainment, media, sports, and lifestyle spaces, advising on contracts, partnerships, intellectual property, marketing agreements, and other matters that protect and advance their commercial interests.
